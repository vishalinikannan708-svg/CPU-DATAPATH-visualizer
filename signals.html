Control & Data Signals

Signals are the communication paths that control and coordinate the flow of information inside a digital logic system. They determine when data is transferred, processed, stored, or passed to another functional unit.

Input Signals

Input signals provide the initial binary information required by the digital system. They may represent user inputs, sensor values, control commands, or data received from another circuit.

Binary Input

Control Signals

Control signals determine how different components operate. They enable or disable specific operations and coordinate the movement of data between system components.

Control Enable

Clock Signal

A clock signal provides a periodic timing reference for synchronous digital systems. Operations occur according to the rising or falling edge of the clock.

Clock Pulse

Output Signals

Output signals contain the final result generated by the digital system. These signals may be sent to another circuit, displayed to the user, or stored for later processing.

Output Data
